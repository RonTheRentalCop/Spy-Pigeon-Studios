What is Tiny Towing?

Your repo company is failing, and your friends are the employees. Take on jobs, haul in vehicles, and work together to turn the business around and take it to new heights.

An early look from the work-in-progress build.

How it plays

  1. 1

    Take on jobs

    Pick up repo orders and decide which ones are worth the trip.

  2. 2

    Haul them in

    Find the car, hook it up and get it back to the yard in one piece.

  3. 3

    Play together

    You and up to five friends share the work, the trucks and the blame.

  4. 4

    Save the business

    Turn a failing company around and take it to new heights.

Coming down the road

Things go wrong
