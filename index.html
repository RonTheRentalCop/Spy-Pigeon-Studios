Installing on Windows
  1. Unzip the whole Suede folder somewhere you like, such as Documents, and keep everything in it together.
  2. Double-click Suede.exe.
  3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the beta isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Suede lives in the system tray as a little pink paw. Right-click it to open Suede or quit.
Installing on a Mac
  1. Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu, choose About This Mac, and look at Chip. "Apple M…" means Apple Silicon. "Intel" means Intel.
  2. Open the downloaded file and drag Suede into Applications.
  3. The first time, macOS may say it can't check Suede because the beta isn't notarised yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Suede.
  4. Suede lives in the menu bar as a little pink paw.

Questions

When can I get Suede?

Right now. The public beta is free to download until 6 November 2026. After that, Suede is coming to Steam.

How much will it cost?

Nothing until 6 November 2026. After that, Suede will be a paid app on Steam, and the price will be shared closer to launch.

Which computers does it work on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit), and Macs with Apple Silicon or Intel chips. Linux is coming soon.

Will it slow my computer down?

Suede is built to stay light. It can also pause the wallpaper while a game or full-screen app is open.

Are Workshop widgets safe?

Yes. Workshop widgets run in a locked sandbox, so they cannot reach your files or the rest of your computer.
