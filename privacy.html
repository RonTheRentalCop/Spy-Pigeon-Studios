Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy covers the Nook app and this website, spypigeonstudios.com, both made by GKoni (Spy Pigeon Studios). It explains what happens to your information when you use them.

In short

  • The Nook app has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.
  • This website counts visits anonymously, without cookies, to see which pages and buttons people use.
  • Everything you set up in Nook stays on your computer.
  • We never sell or share your information, and we don't collect anything that identifies you.

The Nook app

What stays on your computer

Your settings, widgets, sticky notes, plans, checklists, saved setups, scrapbook photos, imported wallpapers, calendar links and log files are all saved on your own computer (in %APPDATA%\Nook on Windows, or ~/Library/Application Support/Nook on a Mac). They are never sent to us. Delete that folder and they're gone.

The record player shows what your computer is already playing, such as Spotify or YouTube. Nook reads this from your computer's own media controls. It isn't recorded or sent anywhere.

When Nook goes online

Nook only connects to the internet to fetch things you ask it to:

  • Wallpapers from a link. When you paste a link from YouTube, TikTok, Tenor, Giphy or another site, Nook downloads that video or picture, or plays it from that site. YouTube videos play through youtube-nocookie.com, YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
  • Calendar sync. When you paste a calendar link (Canvas, Google Classroom, Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar), Nook downloads that calendar about every 30 minutes. It only reads it and never changes anything.
  • Fonts. If a font isn't already on your computer, Nook downloads it from Google Fonts on GitHub.

Like any download, these sites can see your computer's internet address and the request. What they do with it is covered by their own privacy policies: Google and YouTube, TikTok, Tenor, Giphy, GitHub, and your school's or calendar provider's own policy.

The Workshop

Widgets, wallpapers and setups you share are plain files that you send yourself. Nook doesn't upload them anywhere. Shared widgets run in a locked sandbox and can't reach your files.

Tester copies

Tester copies check, on your computer only, that they are being used by the person they were made for. When you sign the Tester Agreement, your name, the date and a code made from your computer's ID are saved inside your copy. None of this is sent to us.

Steam

When Nook is on Steam, Valve handles your Steam account, purchases and downloads under the Steam Privacy Policy. We don't get your payment details.

This website

This website has no cookies, no adverts and no forms. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs such as your internet address for security, under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't see or use those logs.

Visitor statistics

To see how the site is used, we count visits with Umami, a privacy-friendly statistics service. It records which pages are visited, how long visits last, which site you came from, your browser, device type and country, which buttons and links are clicked, which questions are opened and how far down pages people scroll.

Umami doesn't use cookies, doesn't store your internet address, and can't tell who you are or follow you to other sites. We only ever see totals, such as "40 people clicked Join our Discord". If your browser is set to "Do Not Track", you aren't counted at all. See Umami's privacy policy.

The Discord button takes you to Discord, where Discord's privacy policy applies.

Setup photos in the showcase

Photos in the setup exhibit are only shown with the permission of the person who shared them, along with the name they chose (or no name). If a photo of yours is up and you'd like it changed or taken down, ask us on Discord and we'll remove it.

Children

Nook and this website don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Discord requires its users to be at least 13. Tester copies ask testers under 18 to read the Tester Agreement with a parent or guardian.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because our visitor statistics are anonymous and we don't collect personal information, there's normally nothing to hand over, but you can always ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If Nook ever starts collecting anything, we'll say so clearly here first.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests or photo takedowns: message us on the Spy Pigeon Studios Discord.
