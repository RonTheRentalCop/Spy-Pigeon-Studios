Tiny Towing

A co-op game where you and up to five friends run a repo company that's about to go under. Take on jobs, haul in vehicles, and work together to turn the business around and take it to new heights.

Follow development on Discord Back the game

An early look

Golden hour in the valley, a broken-down sedan, and a tow truck that's seen better days. Straight from the work-in-progress build.

Work in progress. Everything you see will get better.

Running a repo company is hard

Especially when it's about to go under and your employees are your friends.

Take on jobs

Pick up repo orders and decide which ones are worth the trip, and the trouble.

Haul them in

Find the car, hook it up and get it back to the yard, hopefully in one piece.

Play together

You and up to five friends share the work, the trucks and the blame.

Save the business

Turn a failing company around and take it to new heights.

What's coming down the road

Being built now. Plans can change as the game takes shape.

  • Things go wrong. Change a flat tyre on the hard shoulder, and hope the semi coming up behind you sees you.
  • Owners fight back. Not everyone wants to hand over their car, and some of them get dangerous.
  • A living town. NPCs going about their day, cops who take an interest, and plenty in between.

Made by high schoolers, paid properly

Everyone making Tiny Towing is a high schooler: the artists, the musicians, the developers and the playtesters. We're doing it the right way, which means paying every one of them for their work.

Real people, real art, real music. No AI.
