Installing on Windows
  1. Unzip the whole Suede folder somewhere you like, such as Documents, and keep everything in it together.
  2. Double-click Suede.exe.
  3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because Suede isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Suede lives in the system tray as a little pink paw. Right-click it to open Suede or quit.
Installing on a Mac
  1. Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu, choose About This Mac, and look at Chip. "Apple M…" means Apple Silicon. "Intel" means Intel.
  2. Open the downloaded file and drag Suede into Applications.
  3. The first time, macOS may say it can't check Suede because Suede isn't notarised by Apple yet. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Suede.
  4. Suede lives in the menu bar as a little pink paw.
